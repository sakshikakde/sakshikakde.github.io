About Me

I'm a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at Fyusion (acquired by Cox Automotive), where I work on automating external damage detection on automobiles, tackling everything from 3D reconstruction to feature matching. Before this, I was an AI/ML Engineer at Quidient, where I deepened my focus on physics-informed rendering and NeRF-like methods for novel view synthesis.

I'm drawn to problems that feel effortless to humans yet remain stubbornly hard for machines. 3D vision is the perfect example.

I strongly believe the best way to truly internalize any concept is to implement it yourself, and when that's not enough, write about it as if you're teaching someone completely new. So I try do both: I build things, wonder why they worked, scratch my head for a while, and then write a blog post pretending I knew what I was doing all along.
